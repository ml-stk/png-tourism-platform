# NTDP Vision and Scope

## Vision

Establish a nationally integrated digital foundation for Papua New Guinea tourism in which the National Tourism Registry is the authoritative source of tourism information and operational workflows, analytics, mapping and distribution consume governed records rather than disconnected spreadsheets or duplicate systems.

## Objectives

- Centralise tourism operator, attraction, product and service records.
- Turn registration, membership, licensing and compliance into controlled workflows.
- Provide tourism intelligence, reporting and forecasting foundations.
- Improve tourism SME visibility and development tracking.
- Provide governed GIS and location intelligence.
- Distribute trusted tourism content through web, mobile/PWA, provincial, kiosk and approved third-party channels.
- Establish secure API and future commerce integration boundaries.
- Preserve auditability, access control and data ownership.

## Scope by phase

### Phase 1 — Registry and regulatory core
Registry, operator lifecycle, licensing/compliance workflow foundations, baseline GIS and analytical foundations.

### Phase 2 — Intelligence, SME/TIA and provincial delivery
Warehouse maturation, analytics dashboards, SME development, TIA membership, compliance audit tooling and provincial/offline channels.

### Phase 3 — Consumer and distribution scale
Super App integration, public API gateway, B2B distribution, live consumer portal and approved third-party integrations.

### Phase 4 — Optimisation and ecosystem integration
Predictive AI, payment integrations, cross-agency hooks and continuous optimisation.

## In scope now

Persistent registry and publication boundary; RBAC and production identity boundary; regulatory lifecycle and audit trail; visitor web/PWA foundations; provincial/kiosk offline manifests and QR handoff; tourism intelligence foundation; governed AI concierge; enterprise baselines for warehouse, GIS, SME, TIA membership, distribution and commerce; versioned APIs and documentation.

## Out of scope for the current baseline

- Migration of legacy production datasets without a controlled migration project.
- Binding the platform to a particular payment provider before PNGTPA selects one.
- Hardware procurement or kiosk installation.
- Full external agency integrations without agreed data-sharing arrangements.
- Predictive models without sufficient governed historical data.
- Treating prototype/demo authentication as production identity.

## Assumptions and dependencies

PNGTPA provides authoritative branding, content and existing operational artefacts. Production identity/SSO decisions are confirmed with PNGTPA/DICT. Hosting, backup and connectivity decisions are subject to production architecture/security review. Payment providers and external integrators are selected by PNGTPA. Data-sharing agreements govern cross-agency integrations.

## Success measures

One governed source for tourism records; traceable registration, membership, licensing and compliance workflows; trusted public publication with provenance; province-aware access; actionable sector analytics; reusable APIs for all five channel classes; auditable security, AI and commerce boundaries.
