# Next-generation product workstreams

With the platform foundation, governed public visitor boundary, offline delivery, intelligence, AI governance, and visitor journey state established, the next product workstream is the **industry ecosystem and operator collaboration layer**.

Priority capabilities:
- operator business profiles distinct from regulatory records
- publishable industry capabilities and experiences
- governed leads/referrals from visitor-facing channels
- operator response state and provenance
- province/operator scoped access controls
- auditability for industry mutations

The industry layer must not expose regulatory or licensing data through public or visitor-facing surfaces. Regulatory records remain authoritative internal data; public business profiles are separately governed and publishable.
