# Visitor visual acceptance target

The visitor homepage follows the approved PNG Tourism Promotion Authority visual reference:

- dark deep-ocean page and navigation surfaces
- official PNG TPA logo as the primary brand lockup
- transparent/immersive top navigation over the hero
- large destination-led hero image
- white and pale-lime headline treatment
- prominent destination/experience/province search
- right-side journey shortcuts: Explore Destinations, Plan Your Journey, Digital Passport, AI Concierge
- featured destination cards with photography
- compact benefit strip for nature, culture, adventure, people, and experiences
- responsive adaptation without reverting to a white SaaS shell

Functional visitor modules remain governed and are not replaced by presentation-only content.
