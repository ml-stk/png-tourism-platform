# Today and Tomorrow — NTDP Operating Model

| Area | Today | Tomorrow with NTDP |
|---|---|---|
| Operator records | Fragmented/manual records | National Registry with authoritative lifecycle |
| Registration/licensing | Manual checks and handoffs | Workflow, role-based review, status and audit |
| Compliance | Manual tracking | Structured compliance status, actions and audit trail |
| Reporting | Manual compilation | Governed facts, snapshots and analytics APIs |
| Small-business visibility | Limited and disconnected | SME profiles, assessments, programmes and public discovery |
| TIA membership | Separate/manual administration | First-class membership records, renewal and fee readiness |
| Mapping | Limited mapping capability | PostGIS-backed tourism geospatial assets and spatial services |
| Distribution | Weak digital distribution | Governed publication and channel distribution queue |
| Data ownership | Multiple sources and copies | Single authoritative registry plus governed public projections |
| Provincial access | Separate/manual processes | Province-scoped administration and offline manifests |
| Visitor channels | Disconnected experiences | Shared services for web, PWA/Super App, provincial, kiosk and third-party channels |
| AI | Not governed as a platform service | Policy-constrained, auditable AI concierge using governed public content |
| Payments | External/manual | Provider-neutral commerce readiness and transaction abstraction |
| Staff work | Re-keying and manual consolidation | Review queues, controlled workflows and dashboards |

## Staff and process change

NTDP shifts staff from repeated data entry and manual consolidation toward validating authoritative records, controlled regulatory decisions, content/publication quality, compliance and renewal monitoring, sector analytics, provincial/channel distribution and integration governance.

## Data ownership model

Registry data is authoritative within the NTDP governance boundary. Regulatory data remains protected from anonymous public access. Published tourism content is the governed distribution dataset. Offline channels cache published content but do not become authoritative. Analytical datasets are derived from governed source events/records and retain provenance.
