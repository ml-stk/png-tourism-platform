# Final visitor interface direction

The visitor experience now follows the approved PNG Tourism Promotion Authority visual direction.

## Brand
- Uses the supplied official PNG TPA logo as a bundled application asset.
- Uses the logo in the visitor header and platform navigation.
- Avoids reconstructing or redrawing the official mark.

## Experience
- White editorial header with destination-focused navigation.
- Immersive ocean, island and tropical visual language in the hero.
- Primary search for destinations, experiences and provinces.
- High-contrast quick actions for destinations, trip planning, digital passport and AI Concierge.
- Featured destination cards consume the governed published destination projection.
- Offline fallback remains visitor-safe and explicitly marked.
- Shared journey section connects itinerary, passport, industry discovery and AI Concierge.

## Engineering boundary
The visual redesign does not bypass API, RBAC, persistence, publication, provenance or AI governance boundaries. Visitor content remains sourced from published public projections and safe local fallback data.

The existing MVP repositories are not modified.
