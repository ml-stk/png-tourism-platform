# Four-Channel Visual Design Prototype Specification

The proposed MVP calls for one common design style across four channel concepts: PNG Tourism Super App, PNG Tourism Website, Provincial Tourism Portals and Tourism Information Kiosks. The current implementation provides a common visual system and responsive visitor experience; this document makes the four acceptance targets explicit.

## Shared design language

- PNGTPA brand identity and approved imagery.
- High-contrast, dark-capable visual foundation suitable for tourism photography.
- Clear typography and accessible form/input contrast.
- Shared cards, navigation, chips, maps, publication indicators and calls to action.
- Progressive disclosure: discovery first for visitor channels; operational density belongs in TPA/admin surfaces.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, mobile/PWA and kiosk orientations.

## PNG Tourism Website — home screen

Hero destination, featured destinations, discover/search, events/experiences, trusted information and AI Concierge entry point. Acceptance: discovery, destination detail, tourism businesses/experiences and AI-assisted discovery without regulatory exposure.

## PNG Tourism Super App — home screen

Personalised discovery, nearby/recommended destinations, itinerary/trip planning, digital passport/QR journeys and offline-friendly content. Acceptance: the app consumes governed visitor/public services and does not maintain an independent tourism source of truth.

## Provincial Tourism Portal — home screen

Province identity, featured local destinations/operators, events, map, provincial information and offline/sync status. Acceptance: administrative scope is province-bound; public content comes from the national publication boundary; stale/offline state is visible.

## Tourism Information Kiosk — home screen/dashboard

Large-touch discovery tiles, map/destination search, featured attractions, QR handoff and connectivity/sync status. Acceptance: kiosk can operate from a versioned national public manifest and hand visitors to public web resources without exposing internal records.

## Design-system compliance

The four channels remain visually related but are not identical. Channel-specific interaction density is allowed while brand, typography, imagery treatment, navigation primitives and public-data trust cues remain shared.
