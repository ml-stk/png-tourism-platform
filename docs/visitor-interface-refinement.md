# Visitor Interface Refinement v1

## Direction

The visitor surface is intentionally darker and more immersive, reducing large white bands between major experiences. The visual language uses PNG's ocean, lagoon, forest and sun tones with strong contrast and restrained glass treatments.

## Brand treatment

The official PNG Tourism Promotion Authority logo is displayed as a dedicated white brand lockup in the visitor header and footer so the supplied mark remains legible against the dark interface.

## Visitor hierarchy

1. Immersive destination hero
2. Featured destinations and governed public projection
3. Industry experiences
4. Journey planning
5. Governed AI Concierge
6. Digital Tourism Passport
7. TPA handoff

The visitor experience remains separate from TPA operational workspaces while sharing platform services and governance boundaries.

## Constraints

- Public destination content remains governed and published-only.
- AI remains behind the governed Concierge API.
- Visitor telemetry remains non-PII and governed.
- No private or regulatory records are exposed to the visitor UI.
- Existing MVP repositories remain untouched.
