# PNG Tourism Platform — Final Interface v1

The visitor experience follows the approved reference direction: official PNG Tourism Promotion Authority branding, a clean editorial header, immersive Pacific-inspired hero, destination-first discovery, prominent journey actions, and a natural green/ocean/gold palette.

## Implemented
- Official supplied PNG TPA logo bundled at `src/assets/png-tpa-logo.png` and used in visitor/platform navigation.
- Visitor homepage information architecture: Destinations, Experiences, Plan Your Trip, Digital Passport, AI Concierge and About PNG.
- Hero search and high-contrast visitor calls to action.
- Featured destination cards connected to the governed public destination projection.
- Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.
- Offline-safe visitor messaging and fallback content.
- Journey strip connecting itinerary, passport, industry discovery and AI Concierge.
- Existing TPA operational workspace remains available behind the platform shell.

## Boundary
This is a presentation-layer evolution. API, persistence, RBAC, publication, provenance, offline, QR and AI governance boundaries remain intact. The existing MVP repositories are untouched.
